About the data
  • Source: the public RDSS dashboard, rdss.powermin.gov.in. A scheduled job opens its state-wise progress pages twice a day and downloads the yearly Excel files it links to. Only public pages are read.
  • Smart metering progress is meters communicating as a share of meters sanctioned. Loss reduction progress is works installed as a share of works sanctioned; it can pass 100% where more was installed than sanctioned.
  • Physical progress is the overall figure RDSS publishes for each state.
  • Funds are in INR crore. GBS is the Gross Budgetary Support (central grant). "GBS released %" is GBS released as a share of GBS sanctioned.
  • AT&C loss, ACS-ARR gap, payable and receivable days are yearly figures from the Integrated Rating, shown for the latest financial year with figures for most states. Lower is better for all four.
  • Ranks count only states with a sanctioned project, or with a reported value for the yearly measures.
